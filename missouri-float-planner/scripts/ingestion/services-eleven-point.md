# Eleven Point River — services research

Five rows, all of them already callable. The work here was checking a federal
agency's own list — and finding that two of its three entries are wrong.

Six rows now. One addition, two corrections, one phone discrepancy left alone.

## The USFS authorized-outfitter list is broken in two places

The Mark Twain National Forest page for the Eleven Point National Wild and
Scenic River lists three authorized canoe rental concessioners. Only one of the
three entries is straightforwardly right.

**"Down River Canoe — https://www.downriver.com/"** is **Downriver Canoe
Company of Bentonville, Virginia**, at 884 Indian Hollow Rd, running trips on
the **Shenandoah River**. No Missouri business of that name could be found on
the Eleven Point in any directory. A federal agency page is linking a Virginia
outfitter 800 miles away as an authorized concessioner for a Missouri river.
Nothing was added for it.

**"Eleven Point River Canoe Rental/Hufstelder's Canoe Rental"** treats two names
as one business with a slash. Hufstedler's own site says plainly it is
"Hufstedler's Canoe Rental, Cabins & Camping" and does **not** trade as Eleven
Point River Canoe Rental. Meanwhile a float directory lists an "Eleven Point
River Canoe Rental" at Rt 2 Box 2522, Alton on **417-778-6497** — a different
number from Hufstedler's 417-778-6116. Either it is a stale listing for
Hufstedler's under prior ownership (the business changed hands in 2024), or it
is a separate business. The slash is not evidence either way, so no alt name was
recorded and no row was added.

That leaves **two** operating liveries on this river, which is what Eddy already
held, and what every independent source agrees on: Hufstedler's for take-outs at
Riverton, Richard's for Greer Crossing. The low outfitter count on the Eleven
Point is the river, not the research — the same conclusion the St. Francis and
Gasconade passes reached.

## Hufstedler's was recorded in the wrong town

The row said **Riverton, 63965**. Hufstedler's own site and the US Census
geocoder both give **33200 US 160, Alton, MO 65606**, and the stored coordinate
already sat within 40 m of that address — so only the town and ZIP were wrong,
not the pin.

63965 is **Van Buren's** ZIP, ninety miles east. That is the same defect this
branch found on the Gasconade, where four rows carried Jerome's 65529, and in
the wider audit, where four Van Buren rows carry Eminence's 65466. It keeps
turning up in rows sourced to the archived business database.

## Greer Crossing Recreation Area

The addition. The Forest Service's developed campground on the Eleven Point and,
by its own description, "one of the most popular launch sites" on the river — 16
single and 3 double sites with tables, fire rings and lantern posts, vault
toilets, drinking water and trash collection from 1 April to 1 November, first
come first served year round at $10 to $20.

It ships **without a coordinate**. Greer Crossing does not resolve in either
geocoder, and Eddy holds two access points about 160 m apart at the same river
mile under different names — "Hwy 19 Bridge" (unapproved) and "Greer Spring"
(approved). Greer Spring is a distinct place from Greer Crossing, so picking
either would be inference rather than evidence. Under the severity split it
warns by name instead, and the pin can follow.

## Left alone: Richard's phone number

The row carries **417-778-6189**, sourced to Eddy's own knowledge base. Two
river directories give **417-778-6186**. `richardscanoerentals.com` returned 503
on every attempt, so the operator could not break the tie.

Elsewhere in this branch the rule has been that a business is the better
authority on how to reach it — that is why Silver Hill's own number replaced the
NPS one. Here the operator is unreachable, so the stored number stands. **One
digit is in question and it should be dialled before it is trusted.**
