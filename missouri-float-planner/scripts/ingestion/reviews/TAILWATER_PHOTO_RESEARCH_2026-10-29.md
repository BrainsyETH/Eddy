# Tailwater access photo research — October 29, 2026

Desk search for reusable photos of the 38 access points on Norfork, White and
Taneycomo, none of which had a photo (issue #1541, section 7). Accepted only
public-domain federal work or Creative Commons photos that can be tied to the
site itself; a generic river view was not enough. Sources: Wikimedia Commons
(name and 1–3 km geosearch), Flickr reusable-licence search, USFWS, USGS, NPS,
USFS, Recreation.gov and USACE pages. Commons rate-limited the search heavily;
DVIDS and OpenStreetMap were unreachable from the research environment.

Accepted entries live in `src/data/reviewed-place-photos.json`, which feeds the
public `/photo-credits` page. A database write fills an empty `image_urls` only.

## Rejected near-misses

- **Bull Shoals-White River State Park Ramp:** CoreBurn's CC BY 2.0 photo of the
  river from the park shoreline. Not geotagged, and the ramp is not in frame.
- **Branson City Ramp / North Beach Park:** CC BY photos geotagged within 200 m,
  but neither shows the launch or any park feature.
- **Dam-Quarry Boat Ramp:** a CC BY-SA photo below the spillway whose geotag is
  an account default; it appears to be closer to the dam than the ramp.
- **Norfork Access:** HAER and Commons photos of the Highway 5 bridge, about
  500 m from the ramp.
- **Buffalo City:** a bluff-top gravel lot 413 m away facing away from the pin.
- **Calico Rock:** a USGS 2008 flood photo with the ramp under water, hosted
  outside the image allowlist.
- **Guion:** Flickr "CC BY" scans of other people's photos of the old ferry;
  the licence is not plausibly the uploader's.
