# Courtois Creek — services research

Courtois had two rows. It now has three, and the interesting work was not
finding new businesses — it was working out which of the Steelville operators
Eddy already holds actually float this creek, and which only appear to.

## The Courtois is a link problem, not a discovery problem

Meramec carries 26 service rows, Huzzah 8, Courtois 2 — and all three are
floated out of the same handful of Steelville resorts. The question is which
operators genuinely run the Courtois, and the answer is narrower than the
directories suggest.

**Confirmed on the operator's own site:**

- **Bass' River Resort** — the Courtois specialist. Its own trip page lists
  6-mile (Blunts to Bass'), 7-mile (Berryman to Blunts, or Bass' to Scotia) and
  13-mile (Berryman to Bass') runs, plus multi-day floats of 20 to 25 miles.
  Already linked.
- **Ozark Outdoors Resort** — its canoeing page names two Courtois routes
  outright: the 10-mile Butts Slab run and a seasonal 5-mile "Courtois
  Primitive". **Link added.**

**Not added, despite a directory saying so:**

- **Garrison's Camping Resort.** A float directory lists it for the Courtois.
  Its own site says "rafts, canoes, kayaks, tubes, & shuttles on the **Meramec
  River**" and names no other water. No link.
- **Huzzah Valley Resort** is already linked to the Courtois in the database,
  but its own site mentions only the Huzzah. The link predates this branch and
  is left alone; it is worth a second look by someone who knows the operation.

## A directory listing that is 150 miles wrong

MCFA's Huzzah/Courtois page lists **Beaver Creek Campground, Cabins and Canoe
Rental** among the three businesses for these creeks. Beaver Creek Campground is
at 159 State Hwy W, **Ava, MO** — on Beaver Creek in Douglas County, roughly 150
miles southwest of Steelville, nearer Bryant Creek and the North Fork.

Adding it would have put a Douglas County campground on a Crawford County creek
on the strength of a trade-directory heading. It is not added, and the near miss
is the argument for checking every directory row against the operator.

## Berryman Campground

The one genuine addition. Eight sites on the footprint of a 1937 CCC camp, at
the trailhead for the Berryman Trail and the Courtois Section of the Ozark
Trail, run by the Mark Twain National Forest. Concrete pads, picnic tables,
lantern posts and fire rings; vault toilets and **no drinking water**; open year
round at $15 a night.

It matters here because it is the upstream put-in: Bass' sells both a 7-mile and
a 13-mile trip that start at Berryman. Eddy already had it as an access point at
Courtois mile 7.06, though an unapproved one; OpenStreetMap and that access point
agree on its position to within 75 m.

## Two more broken records fixed on the way

**Ozark Outdoors Resort's phone was wrong.** The row carried **573-245-6517**;
the operator publishes **573-245-6837**, and so does the archived business
database. That is the third wrong phone number this branch has found in
production, after Buffalo River Float Service and the four uncallable Gasconade
rows.

**Its website was broken too.** The stored `ozarkoutdoors.net` fails TLS
certificate validation — the certificate does not match the hostname, so a
browser refuses the connection. The working address is `ozarkoutdoorsresort.com`.

## Still open on this creek
