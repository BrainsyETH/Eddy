    Safety valves

    The rules that keep the feeds honest and quiet.

    • alertsOnly real crossings into High or Flood. Two or more rivers in 2 hours become one storm digest, not a barrage.
    • clipsClaude vision brand-checks every clip. Flood clips never ride on a fatality or injury.
    • creditCreators are credited, and tagged when their Instagram handle is known.
    • dedupOne post per type, platform and river per day. A clip posts once per platform.
    • TikTokInbox drafts are capped at 5 per 24 hours; Facebook and Instagram still go out.
    Timing, posts, clip counts and the storm are simulated. Route names, cadences and rules come from missouri-float-planner/vercel.json, src/lib/social/, .github/workflows/ and docs/clipengine-ops.md.
