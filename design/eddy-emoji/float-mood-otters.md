# Float recap mood otters: generation prompts

Masters go in `design/eddy-emoji/`; 300px transparent exports go in `eddy-ios/assets/eddy/` via `python3 eddy-ios/scripts/build-eddy-icons.py`, the same as the other emoji.

Generate with the built-in image-generation tool, `transparent_background: true`, using these repository images as character references:

- `design/eddy-emoji/eddy-celebrate.png`
- `design/eddy-emoji/eddy-paddle-ready.png`
- `design/eddy-emoji/eddy-campfire-chill.png`

Each final prompt is the shared direction followed by its subject.

## Shared direction

Use case: mascot sticker. Asset type: Eddy reaction sticker for the float recap card. It is shown at 72–150px on a soft tinted tile and at 200px on a share image. Adapt the supplied Eddy the Otter artwork into one new pose. Keep the established character exactly: navy-indigo body with a bright green chest and face, coral blade sunglasses, the same head shape, whiskers and proportions. Neo-brutalist mascot sticker style: thick near-black outlines, chunky readable shapes, flat colors centered on #163F4A, #F07052, #FAFAFA and #39A0CA. One clear action or prop. Show the character from the waist up or full body, centered, filling about 85% of a square canvas, with a small transparent margin. Real transparent background, including the gaps between limbs and props. No text, letters, numbers, badge, frame, border, drop shadow, gradient, gloss, 3D, detailed scenery or photorealism. Keep it legible at 72px.

## big-water

Primary request: Eddy in a coral kayak riding a big wave train. He is bracing with the paddle in both paws, leaning into it, grinning but clearly gripping hard. Chunky blue wave crests with white spray around the hull. The mood is thrilled but respectful, not reckless. No helmet text and no logos.

## rain

Primary request: Eddy in a coral rain poncho with the hood up, sunglasses still on. He is holding his wooden paddle upright like an umbrella handle, with a small cheerful smile. A few bold blue raindrops and three short diagonal rain strokes around him. No cloud shapes covering the face.

## chilly

Primary request: Eddy bundled up for a cold float. He wears a coral knit beanie and a chunky scarf and holds a steaming mug in both paws, with a small white breath puff by his mouth. He looks cozy and content. No snow and no thermometer.

## scrape

Primary request: Eddy hauling a coral kayak by its bow rope across a few chunky gray-tan gravel stones, leaning forward with a determined, gritted-teeth face. A thin strip of shallow blue water behind the stones. The kayak is shown partly, angled behind him.

## speed-run

Primary request: Eddy mid-stroke with his paddle, sitting in a coral kayak. His fur and whiskers are swept back and his sunglasses are tilted by the wind. Three bold horizontal speed lines trail behind him, plus a small blue wake. A big confident grin.

## optional: quick-dip

Primary request: Eddy sitting on the edge of a coral kayak at the shoreline, dipping one toe into a small blue water ripple and pulling back with a playful, sheepish grin.

## optional: lazy-river

Primary request: Eddy floating on his back in a coral inner tube, paws behind his head, eyes relaxed behind his sunglasses, with a gentle blue ripple around the tube. Fully at ease.
