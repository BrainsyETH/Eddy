# Dam metric icon generation

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool and `transparent_background: true`.
Masters: `eddy-release.png` and `eddy-water-temperature.png` in this directory.
Web exports: `missouri-float-planner/public/icons/` with the same filenames.

Both calls used these repository images as style references:

- `design/eddy-emoji/eddy-water-droplet.png`
- `design/eddy-emoji/eddy-weather.png`
- `eddy-ios/assets/eddy/eddy-dam.png`

Each final prompt was the shared direction followed by its subject below.

## Shared direction

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Eddy app utility icon, a small full-color PNG displayed beside a 12px metric label at about 28–32px. Adapt the supplied utility-icon artwork into a new sibling symbol, preserving its exact visual language: chunky rounded shapes, very thick deep river teal (#163F4A) outlines, bright river blue (#39A0CA) water, sunset coral (#F07052) accent, small warm-white (#FAFAFA) highlights only where useful. Flat neo-brutalist sticker illustration, clean silhouette, solid colors, minimal detail. No mascot, text, letters, numbers, badge, border around the canvas, shadow, gradient, gloss, 3D or extra scenery. Real transparent background, including open gaps; no white or checkerboard backdrop. Square canvas, centered single unified icon, fill about 85% of the canvas with a consistent small transparent margin. Keep it legible at 28px.

## Release

Primary request: replace the reference droplet/dam subjects with a distinct WATER RELEASE symbol. A simple compact coral spillway outlet on the left pours one broad blue stream that bends right into two bold flowing wave bands. Clear directional flow, no arrows or tiny droplets. The water is the main silhouette; the small coral outlet gives the release meaning. Match the reference line weight, rounded corners, and restricted palette exactly. This is one icon, not an icon sheet.

## Water temperature

Primary request: replace the reference droplet/weather subjects with a distinct WATER TEMPERATURE symbol. A chunky upright thermometer with a coral bulb and coral stem sits partly immersed in two short broad blue water waves at its base. Thick teal outer contour, warm-white thermometer body, at most two thick calibration ticks without numbers. A simple compact silhouette that reads instantly as temperature in water, rather than weather or fever. Match the reference line weight, rounded corners, and restricted palette exactly. This is one icon, not an icon sheet.

## iOS Inflow and fishery icons

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool and `transparent_background: true`, using `design/eddy-emoji/eddy-water-temperature.png` and `eddy-ios/assets/eddy/eddy-other-usgs-gauge.png` as style references. Masters are `eddy-inflow.png` and `eddy-fishery.png`; bundled exports use the same filenames under `eddy-ios/assets/eddy/`.

Each prompt uses this shared direction followed by its subject:

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Eddy app utility symbol for a small metric label, displayed at 28px. Adapt the supplied Eddy utility icon artwork into one new matching symbol. Preserve the established thick deep teal (#163F4A) outlines, bright blue (#39A0CA), coral (#F07052), optional warm-white highlight, chunky rounded simple shapes and flat neo-brutalist sticker style. Real transparent background and open gaps, centered single unified icon with a small margin, square canvas. Keep the silhouette legible at 28px. No text, numbers, mascot, badge, scenery, gradient, gloss, 3D or drop shadow.

### inflow

Primary request: a WATER INFLOW symbol. One bold coral downward arrow enters a compact basin of two broad blue wave bands. The arrow points into the water, clearly showing water entering a lake. Simple cohesive silhouette with very thick teal contours. No pipes, dam, extra arrows, small droplets or thin decorative marks.

### fishery

Primary request: a FISHERY symbol for the water below a dam. A single simple side-view fish, facing right, with a broad blue body, coral tail and dorsal fin, thick teal outline and one large round teal eye. Generic fish rather than a realistic identifiable species; this marks either trout water or warm water. No hook, fishing rod, extra waves, scales, spots or detailed gills.
