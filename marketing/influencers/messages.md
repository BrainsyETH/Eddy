# Creator outreach — message templates

Plan and rules: `README.md`. Every message goes out from you, by name.
Replace `[brackets]`. **The `[specific]` line is not optional.** It's one
sentence about a real video or river of theirs, and most of the reply rate
comes from it.

Keep DMs short. Platforms truncate long first messages, and a wall of text
from a stranger reads like a pitch.

---

## 1a. First message — cold (DM)

> Hey [Name], [specific: "your Jacks Fork float from Alley Spring in June was
> the best look at that stretch I've seen"].
>
> I'm [Your name], and I built Eddy (eddy.guide), a free app for Ozarks
> floaters: live gauge readings turned into a plain "is it floatable?" rating,
> plus put-ins, float times and shuttle info for [24] rivers.
>
> I'd love to give you a free year of Eddy Premium in exchange for honest
> feedback. No post required, I just want people who are actually on these
> rivers telling me what's wrong. Interested?

*(Check the live river count at eddy.guide/coverage before sending. Don't
copy the number from here.)*

## 3. They said yes — the code

> Awesome, thank you! Here's your code: **[CODE]**
>
> Two steps, in this order:
> 1. Install Eddy (https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794933267), open the
>    **Settings** tab, and **Sign in with Apple**. Premium attaches to your
>    account, so this has to come first.
> 2. On that same screen tap **Redeem code**, enter the code on Apple's
>    screen, then come back to Eddy. You should see "Premium is active".
>
> Heads-up so nothing surprises you: it's a free year on the normal annual
> subscription, so it renews at the regular price after 12 months unless you
> cancel. You can turn that off in iPhone Settings → your name →
> Subscriptions anytime.
>
> What helps most:
> - **On the water:** if a rating doesn't match what you're seeing, tap
>   *"Didn't match the river? Tell Eddy"* at the bottom of the river screen.
>   It sends me the exact gauge and reading, which is gold.
> - **Anything else:** just reply here, even one-liners. "This confused me" is
>   as useful as "this is broken."
>
> I'll check in in a couple of weeks. And if you ever do post about Eddy,
> please mention it was a free subscription. Totally optional, though.

*(Android users: Eddy is iPhone-only right now. Offer the website,
eddy.guide, which is free with no code needed, and still ask for feedback.)*

## 4. Feedback check-in (+10–14 days)

> Hey [Name], checking in on Eddy. Five quick ones, and answer any you like:
>
> 1. Did you open it before a float? Which river?
> 2. Did the rating match what you found on the water?
> 3. What's the first thing that confused or annoyed you?
> 4. Is the daily report worth paying for, or is the free app enough?
> 5. What would make you tell a friend about it?
>
> Voice memo is fine if that's easier.

*(Question 4 is the one we can't get any other way. A creator with a free
year has no reason to be polite about the price.)*

---

## 5. Thank-you — close the loop

> Thank you, this is exactly what I needed. [What changed: "You were right

>
> If anything else looks off, you know where to find me.

*Only send this once something has actually changed, or you can say exactly
when it will. "Noted!" doesn't count.*
